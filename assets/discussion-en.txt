Voix égales — Discussion guide

Our position: we support adopting proportional representation in Québec. The detailed rules remain open to discussion.

1. Why do we want seat shares to better reflect vote shares?

2. How do thresholds and constituency size affect representation?

3. How can local representation be retained while correcting gaps between votes and seats?

4. What rules would be needed for ties, scrutiny and recounts?

5. How could a reform be evaluated using evidence, multiple scenarios and public consultation?
